How SpreadPay Works

One annual premium. Flexible ways to pay.

Once your SpreadPay™ transaction is successfully completed, the annual premium is settled to your chosen participating insurer in accordance with the partnership arrangement. You then make your agreed SpreadPay payments over the applicable period.

Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.

Why it matters

  • You stay insured for the policy period.
  • The annual premium is paid to your chosen participating insurer.
  • Payments you can plan and manage over time.
  • Your terms are presented to you before you agree.

Your SpreadPay Journey

From choosing your insurer to making your agreed payments — here is the complete 6-step journey.

  1. 1

    Choose Your Insurance

    Choose an eligible motor insurance policy from a participating insurer, broker or authorised agent.

  2. 2

    Ask for Moovon SpreadPay™

    Tell the participating partner that you would like to use Moovon SpreadPay™.

  3. 3

    Complete Your Details

    Complete the required application and identity and eligibility information.

  4. 4

    Pay Your Agreed Deposit

    Pay the agreed initial deposit shown as part of your SpreadPay arrangement.

  5. 5

    Your Insurer Activates the Policy

    Your participating insurer completes the insurance process and activates your policy.

  6. 6

    Make Your Flexible Payments

    Pay the remaining balance through flexible payments over the agreed period.

Responsible Payment

Responsible & Flexible Payment

SpreadPay™ is designed to be cleared, not stretched. The more you pay upfront, the less you have left to spread.

PAY MORE. FINISH SOONER.

You can always pay more than the minimum deposit or scheduled amount. The more you pay upfront, the less you have left to spread.

Higher deposit upfront → Lower remaining balance → Balance cleared sooner

Mr. Moovon's tip

Pay more when you can. Clear early when you can. Keep your SpreadPay account on track.

CLEAR YOUR BALANCE. STAY PENALTY-FREE.

Where the full outstanding balance is cleared within the applicable 4-week payment period, no late-payment penalty applies.

Your balance → 4-week payment period → Balance cleared
If a balance remains outstanding beyond the applicable payment period, late-payment charges may apply in accordance with your SpreadPay™ agreement.
