Who does what

Three parties, three clear roles — so you always know who to speak to about what.

Insurance Company

Provides and underwrites your insurance policy. Participating insurers are licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana.

Moovon SpreadPay™

Facilitates the customer journey, account administration and the payment experience. Moovon is not an insurer and does not handle claims.

Licensed & Regulated Financial Institution

Provides or participates in the applicable premium-financing arrangement, in partnership with Moovon.

Moovon does not choose an insurance company for you, and does not handle insurance claims. Policy and claim matters remain with your participating insurer. Eligibility, product availability and applicable terms and conditions apply.
