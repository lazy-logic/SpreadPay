The 100% Upfront Settlement Principle

How SpreadPay Works for Drivers & Insurers

A simple, automated journey from instant customer verification to full 100% annual premium remittance.

5-Step Automated Journey

From Onboarding to Full Settlement

Every step is engineered for maximum speed, security, and absolute clarity.

1

Customer Verified

Identity and credit checks are completed in seconds via Ghana Card and biometric liveness selfie verification.

2

Deposit & Fees Paid

Customer pays the required initial deposit and applicable setup fee instantly via Mobile Money or debit card.

3

Transaction Completed

SpreadPay confirms the finance agreement and generates the electronic policy verification token.

4

100% Premium Settled

Moovon remits 100% of the annual premium upfront directly to the insurance company's nominated account.

5

Repayment Administered

Customer repays the financed balance in affordable installments under the SpreadPay agreement.

Operational Clarity

Insurance Partner vs. Moovon Position

Operational Area Insurance Partner Position Moovon SpreadPay Position
Underwriting & Issuance Underwrites and issues the policy; assumes insurance risk; issues the insurance certificate sticker. Provides quote calculator API, customer onboarding portal, and eligibility engine.
Premium Settlement Receives 100% of the annual premium in a single lump-sum settlement. Disburses full premium to the insurer once customer deposit and setup fees clear.
Collections & Administration Zero collection burden or credit follow-up required from your agents. Administers customer repayments, reminders, automated MoMo debits, and KYC audits.

Swipe the table sideways to see every column.

Wording Note: Customer repayments arise under the separate SpreadPay finance agreement and do not defer, reduce, or otherwise affect the insurer's entitlement to the annual premium.
