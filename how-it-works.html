SpreadPay Protected™

One Annual Premium. Flexible Ways to Pay.

Moovon SpreadPay™ helps eligible customers finance their motor insurance premium through arrangements with licensed and regulated financial institutions.

Find a Participating Partner

Your annual premium is paid. Your payments are spread.

Insurance is provided and underwritten by participating insurance companies licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana. Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions. Eligibility, product availability and applicable terms and conditions apply.

Your SpreadPay Journey

  1. 1

    Choose Your Insurance

    Select an eligible motor insurance policy from a participating insurer, broker or authorised agent.

  2. 2

    Ask for SpreadPay™

    Tell your participating partner you would like to use Moovon SpreadPay™.

  3. 3

    Complete Your Details

    Complete the required application, identity and eligibility information.

  4. 4

    Pay Your Agreed Deposit

    Pay the applicable upfront amount presented as part of your SpreadPay arrangement.

  5. 5

    Your Insurer Activates Your Policy

    Your participating insurer activates and issues your policy. Insurance is provided and underwritten by the insurer.

  6. 6

    Make Your Agreed Payments

    Manage the remaining SpreadPay balance according to your agreed terms.

Roles & Responsibilities

Who Does What?

Three parties. Three clear roles. Here is exactly who is responsible for what across your SpreadPay journey.

Insurance Company

Provides and underwrites the insurance policy.

  • Underwrites your policy
  • Issues and activates your policy
  • Manages your insurance cover
  • Handles policy and claim matters

Participating insurers are licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana.

Moovon SpreadPay™

Facilitates the customer journey, account administration and payment experience.

  • Digital customer journey
  • SpreadPay account administration
  • Payment experience and reminders
  • Customer support via Mr. Moovon’s HelpDesk

Moovon is not an insurance company and does not handle insurance claims.

Licensed & Regulated Financial Institution

Provides or participates in the applicable premium-financing arrangement.

  • Provides or participates in the premium financing
  • Applies the financing terms and requirements
  • Supports responsible financing practices

Eligibility and applicable terms are determined under the relevant financing arrangement.

Your insurer. Your policy. Your choice. Moovon does not choose an insurance company for the customer. Choose your preferred participating insurer and ask for Moovon SpreadPay™.
Good to Know

Before You Agree

A few things worth understanding before you complete a SpreadPay arrangement — and who to talk to about what.

Your policy is provided and underwritten by the participating insurance company you choose. Participating insurers are licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana. Moovon is not an insurance company.
Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions. Moovon SpreadPay™ facilitates the journey, your account administration and the payment experience.
Your applicable deposit, fees, payment schedule and total amount payable should be presented to you before you complete the relevant SpreadPay agreement. Take the time to read it before you agree.
No. Moovon does not handle insurance claims. Policy and claim matters remain with your participating insurer. For SpreadPay account or payment questions, Mr. Moovon’s HelpDesk is here to help.
