Integration Capabilities

What you can build with

A high-level view of what approved partners can connect to. Technical specifications are shared privately once your organisation is approved.

  • Customer Onboarding

    Start a SpreadPay application from inside your own journey and capture the application, identity and eligibility information your arrangement requires.

  • Premium Financing

    Create and manage SpreadPay arrangements for eligible customers. Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.

  • Payments

    Present the agreed deposit and payment schedule to the customer, and follow the status of payments made against a SpreadPay account.

  • Policy & Settlement Status

    Confirm that the participating insurer has activated or issued the policy, and follow the status of the related SpreadPay transaction.

  • Webhooks & Notifications

    Subscribe to event notifications so your systems stay in step with the key updates on a SpreadPay arrangement, without polling for changes.

  • Reports & Insights

    Retrieve partner reporting to support your own operations, service levels and performance review. Detailed operational data stays in the secure portal.

Role clarity: Moovon provides the technology, customer journey and administration layer. Insurance is provided and underwritten by participating insurance companies licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana. Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions. Moovon is not an insurer and does not handle insurance claims — policy and claim matters remain with the customer's participating insurer.
Eligibility

Who can integrate

Developer access is granted to approved organisations only, subject to due diligence and the applicable partnership arrangement.

  • Insurance Companies

    Participating insurers connecting policy issuance and the SpreadPay journey to their own systems.

  • Licensed & Regulated Financial Institutions

    Institutions that provide or participate in the applicable premium-financing arrangement.

  • Brokers & Aggregators

    Brokers, authorised agents and aggregators distributing eligible motor insurance products.

  • Approved Technology Partners

    Platforms and service providers building on behalf of an approved insurance or financing partner.

Security & Reliability

Secure. Reliable. Partner ready.

Everything technical is issued privately. Nothing that could expose a customer, a partner or our infrastructure is published on this page.

What you can rely on

  • Secure authentication and encrypted communication between your systems and ours.
  • A sandbox environment so approved partners can test an integration before going live.
  • Documentation and worked examples issued to your named technical contacts.
  • Monitored infrastructure with operational support for approved partners.
  • Advance change notices, so you hear from us before anything affects your integration.
  • A named integration contact for questions during build and after go-live.
