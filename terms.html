The key terms of Moovon SpreadPay™ premium financing, in plain language.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

How to read this page. This is a plain-language summary of how Moovon SpreadPay™ works, published so you can review the key terms before you apply. The binding agreement is the SpreadPay financing agreement you accept at sign-up, together with the motor insurance policy issued by your chosen insurer. Where the two differ, the signed agreement and your policy wording apply.

1. What SpreadPay is

Moovon SpreadPay™ is a premium financing service. When you use it, Moovon pays 100% of your annual motor insurance premium to your chosen insurance partner upfront, and you repay Moovon in installments. Moovon does not underwrite insurance and is not your insurer — your cover is issued and your claims are handled by the licensed partner insurer you choose.

2. Who can apply

  • You must hold a valid Ghana Card, verified through the NIA, and pass a live selfie check.
  • You must buy your motor policy from one of Moovon’s participating partner insurers.
  • The annual premium being financed must be GH₵330 or more.
  • You must have a mobile money wallet or card able to make the deposit and repayments.

All fees are disclosed to you before you accept the agreement. Your deposit and setup fee are payable at sign-up; the financed balance is what you repay to Moovon.

4. Late payment

Each installment has a four-week payment window. If an installment is still unpaid after that window, a late penalty is added for that month:

  • Essential: GH₵50 flat for each unpaid month.
  • Flex: a 3.5% flat monthly penalty, calculated on the original financed amount. For example, on a GH₵4,000 financed balance that is GH₵140 for that month.
  • Fixed: no monthly penalty during the 3 fixed repayments. If the balance is still in default after month 3, a 4% flat monthly penalty applies for up to 6 further months (maximum term 9 months).

Settling early stops any further monthly penalties immediately.

5. If the plan is not repaid

Your policy is fully active from day one and your insurer handles your claims normally while you are repaying. However, if your account remains in default beyond the maximum term for your tier, Moovon may end the financing agreement and request cancellation of the policy with your insurer. Any unused premium is then treated according to your policy wording and your signed agreement. Cancellation means you would no longer be insured, so please contact us before you miss a payment — we would rather rearrange than cancel.

6. Early settlement

You may clear your outstanding balance at any time. Early settlement is encouraged and immediately stops any further monthly penalties.

7. Your insurer’s role and ours

Your partner insurer is responsible for underwriting, issuing your policy and certificate, and settling claims. Moovon is responsible for financing the premium, collecting your repayments, and administering your SpreadPay plan. Questions about cover or a claim go to your insurer; questions about payments and your plan come to us.

8. Larger facilities

A financed balance above GH₵50,000 requires approval from the partner insurer’s Relationship Manager before the plan can be activated.

9. Changes to these terms

Moovon may update the published tiers, fees and this summary. Changes do not alter the terms of a SpreadPay agreement you have already entered into — those remain as accepted at sign-up.
