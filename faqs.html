Ask Mr. Moovon on WhatsApp

Moovon SpreadPay™ is a smart motor premium financing platform in Ghana. It allows drivers and fleet owners to spread their Third Party, Comprehensive, or Commercial motor insurance payments into affordable monthly installments, while the insurance partner receives 100% of the annual premium upfront.

You can view your exact monthly breakdown anytime on our Repayment Calculator.

Onboarding is 100% digital and takes under 60 seconds. All you need is:

  • A valid Ghana Card (National Identification Authority).
  • A live biometric selfie for instant liveness verification.
  • An active Mobile Money wallet (MTN, Telecel, AT) or debit card for the deposit.

We support all major payment channels across Ghana, including MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, as well as Visa and Mastercard debit/credit cards. Monthly installments are debited automatically with automated SMS and WhatsApp reminders prior to each due date.

Late payment penalties depend on your product tier:

  • Essential Tier: GH₵50 flat monthly penalty if unpaid after 4 weeks.
  • Flex Tier: 3.5% flat monthly penalty for each month an installment is overdue, calculated on the original financed amount (e.g. GH₵140 on a GH₵4,000 financed balance).
  • Fixed Tier: No monthly penalty during the 3 fixed repayments. If the balance is still in default after Month 3, a 4.0% flat monthly penalty applies for up to 6 further months (9 months maximum).

Timely repayments keep your SpreadPay credit rating high for future vehicle financing.

Yes, absolutely. Early settlement is strongly encouraged and incurs zero prepayment penalties. Paying early closes out your balance immediately and unlocks higher financing limits for additional private or commercial vehicles.

Under the core 100% Upfront Settlement Principle, once the customer completes verification and pays the initial deposit and setup fee, Moovon disburses the full 100% annual premium directly into the insurance partner's nominated bank account. The insurer carries zero deferred premium exposure.

For full settlement rules and reconciliation procedures, see the Insurance Partner Operations Guide.

The Insurance Partner (such as Enterprise, Hollard, Activa, Glico, Star Assurance, etc.) underwrites the risk, issues the policy, and provides the official National Insurance Commission (NIC) sticker. Moovon acts strictly as the premium financing technology and collections partner.

Because your insurer receives 100% of the annual premium upfront, your policy is 100% fully active and comprehensive from Day 1. You file insurance claims directly with your insurance company following their standard claims procedures. SpreadPay installment financing does not delay or invalidate your claims.

Under the Fixed Tier, the platform automatically finances balances up to GH₵50,000. Any corporate fleet or high-value vehicle transaction exceeding GH₵50,000 financed balance triggers our automated escalation protocol and requires RM Approval (Relationship Manager sign-off) from the Insurance Partner before the policy can be issued.

Moovon manages 100% of customer collections, automated MoMo debits, SMS payment reminders, and KYC audits. Your insurance agents and branch managers are relieved of all collection burdens, enabling them to focus entirely on underwriting and customer acquisition.
