Moovon SpreadPay™ is a premium-financing journey for eligible annual motor insurance. You choose a participating insurer, ask for SpreadPay™, complete your details and pay your agreed deposit. Your insurer then activates or issues your policy, and you make your agreed payments over the applicable period.

Your annual premium is paid. Your payments are spread. Moovon provides the technology, customer journey and account administration — insurance comes from your insurer, and financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.

See the six-step SpreadPay journey

Your insurance is provided and underwritten by the participating insurance company you choose — an insurer licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana.

Moovon is not an insurer and does not underwrite policies. Your cover, your policy documents and your certificate come from your insurer, and the terms of your policy are between you and that insurer.

Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions. The applicable financing arrangement is provided by, or participated in by, that regulated institution.

Moovon SpreadPay™ facilitates the customer journey, account administration and payment experience that connect your chosen insurer with the applicable premium-financing arrangement.

Yes. Your insurer. Your policy. Your choice.

Moovon does not choose an insurance company for the customer, and we do not rank, score or recommend participating insurers. Browse the participating network, decide which insurer is right for you, and ask them for Moovon SpreadPay™.

SpreadPay™ is designed for eligible annual motor insurance policies offered by participating insurers, covering private, commercial and fleet motor needs.

Whether a particular policy is eligible depends on the insurer, the product and the information provided in your application. Your participating partner will confirm eligibility with you before you agree to anything.

At step 3 of your journey you complete the required application, identity and eligibility information. Your participating partner will tell you exactly what is needed for your chosen insurer and product, as requirements can differ between insurers and products.

Only share your details through your participating partner or an official Moovon channel.

You pay an agreed deposit — the applicable upfront amount presented as part of your SpreadPay™ arrangement.

For SpreadPay Essential™ the minimum upfront payment starts from GH₵125, and you are welcome to pay a larger deposit. The more you pay upfront, the less you have left to spread.

Your applicable deposit, fees, payment schedule and total amount payable should be presented to you before you complete the relevant SpreadPay™ agreement.

Your payments depend on your eligible annual premium, the deposit you pay and the arrangement you agree:

  • SpreadPay Essential™ — annual premiums of GH₵330–GH₵1,500: pay your agreed deposit and spread the remaining balance over up to 3 months.
  • SpreadPay Flex™ — annual premiums of GH₵1,501–GH₵15,000: eligible customers can spread payments over up to 6 months, subject to applicable terms.
  • SpreadPay Fixed™ — eligible larger premiums: structured payment arrangements are available for eligible customers, subject to applicable terms.

The figures that apply to you are calculated on your own arrangement and set out in your SpreadPay™ agreement, so you can see the full picture before you commit.

Role clarity

Who does what?

Three parties, three clear roles. Knowing which one to speak to saves you time.

Your insurance company

Provides and underwrites your insurance policy, issues your cover, and handles all policy and claim matters. Licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana.

Moovon SpreadPay™

Facilitates the customer journey, account administration and the payment experience — and answers your SpreadPay account and payment questions.

Licensed & regulated financial institution

Provides or participates in the applicable premium-financing arrangement that lets your payments be spread over the agreed period.

Moovon does not handle insurance claims. Policy and claim matters remain with the customer's participating insurer. For SpreadPay account or payment questions, Mr. Moovon's HelpDesk is here to help — see who to contact for what.
