About Moovon

Moving Ghana Forward.

Built in Ghana. Built for Africa.

Moovon is a Ghanaian technology company transforming how motorists access and pay for annual motor insurance through a connected premium-financing ecosystem.

Your insurer. Your choice. Flexible ways to pay.

Why We Exist

Insurance shouldn’t stop you moving.

Motor insurance is essential, but paying an annual premium upfront can place pressure on individuals, drivers and businesses.

We built SpreadPay™ to help bridge that gap — enabling eligible customers to have the applicable annual premium paid to their chosen participating insurer while managing their SpreadPay payments over time.

Your annual premium is paid. Your payments are spread.

What Moovon Does

We connect the journey.

Four parties, four clearly separate roles — so everyone knows exactly who does what.

Participating Insurance Companies

Provide and underwrite the customer’s insurance policy. Insurance is provided and underwritten by participating insurance companies licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana.

Moovon SpreadPay™

Provides the technology, customer journey and administration that connect insurance with premium financing. Moovon is not an insurer and does not handle claims.

Licensed & Regulated Financial Institutions

Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions, which provide or participate in the applicable arrangement.

Customers

Remain free to choose from participating insurance companies and eligible insurance products. The choice of insurer always belongs to the customer.

Insurance policies are provided and underwritten by participating insurance companies licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana. Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions. Moovon does not handle insurance claims — policy and claim matters remain with your participating insurer.

Mission & Vision

Why we do it — and where we’re going.

Our Mission

To make annual motor insurance more accessible by connecting customers, insurers and regulated financial institutions through simple technology and responsible premium financing.

Our Vision

To build Africa’s leading technology-enabled premium-financing ecosystem for motor insurance.

Our Journey

Ghana Today. Africa Tomorrow.

Our journey starts in Ghana. Our ambition is African.

Moovon was built in Ghana with a simple ambition: to make annual motor insurance easier to access, manage and pay for. We are building SpreadPay™ to grow across African markets while working with local insurers, regulated financial institutions and distribution partners in each market.

  1. Ghana

    Our home. Where SpreadPay™ is live with participating insurance partners today.

  2. West Africa

    Our next chapter, built with local insurers and regulated financial institutions in each market.

  3. Africa

    Our future — a premium-financing ecosystem designed to grow from Ghana into markets across the continent.
