Simple Customer Journey

Six Simple Steps

From choosing your insurance to making your agreed payments — here is how a SpreadPay™ journey works.

  1. 1

    Choose your insurance

    Select an eligible motor insurance policy from a participating insurer, broker or authorised agent.

  2. 2

    Ask for SpreadPay™

    Tell your participating partner you would like to use Moovon SpreadPay™.

  3. 3

    Complete your details

    Complete the required application, identity and eligibility information.

  4. 4

    Pay your agreed deposit

    Pay the applicable upfront amount presented as part of your SpreadPay arrangement.

  5. 5

    Your insurer issues your policy

    Your insurance is provided and underwritten by the participating insurer you chose.

  6. 6

    Make your agreed payments

    Manage the remaining SpreadPay balance according to your agreed terms.

Core Benefits

Your Annual Premium Is Paid. Your Payments Are Spread.

SpreadPay™ is built around one idea: keep eligible motor cover within reach, and let payments fit the way you earn.

  • Your premium is paid

    Your annual premium is paid and your payments are spread over the applicable period. Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.

  • Flexible payment options

    Flexible payment options for eligible customers. Your applicable deposit, payment schedule and total amount payable are presented before you agree.

  • Simple digital journey

    A simple digital journey — apply with your participating partner and manage your SpreadPay account online.

  • Real support

    Real customer support through Mr. Moovon’s HelpDesk for your SpreadPay journey, account and payments.

Flexible Ways to Pay

Options Built Around Your Premium

Which option applies depends on your annual premium. Eligibility, product availability and applicable terms and conditions apply.

  • SpreadPay Essential™

    Annual premiums GH₵330 – GH₵1,500

    Pay your agreed deposit and spread the remaining balance over up to 3 months.

  • SpreadPay Flex™

    Annual premiums GH₵1,501 – GH₵15,000

    Eligible customers can spread payments over up to 6 months, subject to applicable terms.

  • SpreadPay Fixed™

    Eligible larger annual premiums

    Structured payment arrangements are available for eligible customers, subject to applicable terms.
