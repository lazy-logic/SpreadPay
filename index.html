How It Works

  1. 1

    Choose Your Insurance

    Choose the right motor insurance policy from your preferred insurer, broker or agent.

  2. 2

    Ask for Moovon SpreadPay™

    Tell them you want to pay with Moovon SpreadPay™. They’ll guide you through the process.

  3. 3

    Pay Your Deposit

    Make the agreed deposit. We pay the full premium to your insurance company.

  4. 4

    Drive SpreadPay Protected™.

    Your policy is active. You repay over time with flexible payments.
