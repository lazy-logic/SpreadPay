How SpreadPay Works

A seamless journey from instant digital onboarding to full annual policy remittance.

1

Customer Verified

Ghana Card NIA check and live selfie verified in under 60 seconds.

2

Deposit & Fees Paid

Customer pays initial deposit and setup fee via Mobile Money or Card.

3

Transaction Completed

SpreadPay agreement confirms and insurance verification token issues.

4

100% Premium Settled

Moovon remits 100% of the annual premium upfront to the insurer.

5

Repayment Administered

Customer repays financed balance in affordable monthly installments.
