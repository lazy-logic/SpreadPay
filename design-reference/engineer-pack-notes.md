Implementation Notes
  - Use the approved homepage as the main visual benchmark across the site.
  - Keep Mr. Moovon consistent as a character, but do not overcrowd every page with him.
  - Do not introduce green into Moovon’s core brand palette; third-party brand marks may retain their own colours.
  - Insurance is provided/underwritten by participating insurers. Moovon should not be presented as handling claims.
  - Premium financing should be described as being provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.
  - Customers choose their participating insurer. Moovon should not recommend or decide the insurer for them.
  - Rates, fees and changing product parameters should not be unnecessarily hard-coded into public marketing pages.
  - Real API documentation, credentials, endpoints and internal infrastructure must remain behind authenticated developer/partner access.
  - A compact ‘Follow Mr. Moovon’ social section should be used consistently across public pages.
