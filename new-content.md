 02 — About Moovon
Hero
•	Moving Ghana Forward.
•	Built in Ghana. Built for Africa.
•	Moovon is a Ghanaian technology company transforming how motorists access and pay for annual motor insurance through a connected premium-financing ecosystem.
Why We Exist
•	Motor insurance is essential, but paying an annual premium upfront can place pressure on individuals, drivers and businesses.
•	We built SpreadPay™ to help bridge that gap — enabling eligible customers to have the applicable annual premium paid to their chosen participating insurer while managing their SpreadPay payments over time.
What Moovon Does
•	Participating insurance companies provide and underwrite the customer’s insurance policy.
•	Moovon SpreadPay™ provides the technology, customer journey and administration connecting insurance with premium financing.
•	Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.
•	Customers remain free to choose from participating insurance companies and eligible insurance products.
Mission & Vision
•	Our mission is to make annual motor insurance more accessible by connecting customers, insurers and regulated financial institutions through simple technology and responsible premium financing.
•	Our vision is to build Africa’s leading technology-enabled premium-financing ecosystem for motor insurance.
Growth
•	Our journey starts in Ghana. Our ambition is African.
•	We are building SpreadPay™ to grow across African markets while working with local insurers, regulated financial institutions and distribution partners in each market.
 03 — How SpreadPay Works
Hero
•	One Annual Premium. Flexible Ways to Pay.
•	Moovon SpreadPay™ helps eligible customers finance their motor insurance premium through arrangements with licensed and regulated financial institutions.
Customer Journey
•	1. Choose your insurance — Select an eligible motor insurance policy from a participating insurer, broker or authorised agent.
•	2. Ask for SpreadPay™ — Tell your participating partner you would like to use Moovon SpreadPay™.
•	3. Complete your details — Complete the required application, identity and eligibility information.
•	4. Pay your agreed deposit — Pay the applicable upfront amount presented as part of your SpreadPay arrangement.
•	5. Your insurer activates/issues your policy — Insurance is provided and underwritten by the participating insurer.
•	6. Make your agreed payments — Manage the remaining SpreadPay balance according to your agreed terms.
Who Does What?
•	Insurance Company — Provides and underwrites the insurance policy.
•	Moovon SpreadPay™ — Facilitates the customer journey, account administration and payment experience.
•	Licensed & Regulated Financial Institution — Provides or participates in the applicable premium-financing arrangement.
Key Line
•	Your annual premium is paid. Your payments are spread.
 04 — For Customers
Hero
•	Keep Moving.
•	Your annual premium is paid. Your payments are spread.
•	Choose your preferred participating insurer and ask for Moovon SpreadPay™.
Flexible Ways to Pay
•	Annual premiums GH₵330–GH₵1,500 (SpreadPay Essential™): pay your agreed deposit and spread the remaining balance over up to 3 months.
•	Minimum upfront payment from GH₵125 for Essential. Customers may choose to pay a larger deposit.
•	Annual premiums GH₵1,501–GH₵15,000 (SpreadPay Flex™): eligible customers can spread payments over up to 6 months, subject to applicable terms.
•	Eligible larger premiums (SpreadPay Fixed™): structured payment arrangements are available for eligible customers, subject to applicable terms.
Responsible Payment Message
•	The more you pay upfront, the less you have left to spread.
•	Clear your applicable full outstanding balance within the relevant 4-week payment period and no late-payment penalty applies.
•	If a balance remains outstanding beyond the applicable period, late-payment charges may apply in accordance with your SpreadPay agreement.
•	Pay more when you can. Clear early when you can. Keep your SpreadPay account on track.
Know Before You Agree
•	Your applicable deposit, fees, payment schedule and total amount payable should be presented before you complete the relevant SpreadPay agreement.
Choice
•	Your insurer. Your policy. Your choice.
•	Moovon does not choose an insurance company for the customer.
