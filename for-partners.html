Insurance Partner Product & Operations Guide

Confidential operational reference for existing insurance partners, approved brokers, and underwriting agents.

1. Executive Summary & Core Settlement Principle

SpreadPay enables eligible customers to spread insurance premium payments while the insurance partner receives 100% of the annual premium upfront in a single settlement following completion of the transaction.

Core Principle: The product choice affects the customer finance arrangement only. It does not defer, reduce, or alter the insurer's receipt of the 100% annual premium.
Component Operational Standard
What SpreadPay does Enables eligible customers to spread insurance premium payments into manageable monthly installments.
What stays unchanged The insurer settlement model remains unchanged: 100% of the annual premium is settled to the insurer.
Why it matters Removes affordability barriers, increases conversion & retention, and provides brokers with clear financing tools.

3. Product Positioning & Underwriting Limits

Product Annual Premium Designed For Key Structure
Essential GH₵330 – GH₵1,500 Qualifying customers; primarily Third Party motor Minimum GH₵100 deposit + GH₵25 Setup Fee; up to 3 months
Flex GH₵1,501 – GH₵15,000 Private & commercial motor seeking flexibility 20% deposit; 2% Setup Fee; up to 6 months; 3.5% monthly late penalty
Fixed GH₵15,001 – GH₵50,000 Larger premiums; fleets and corporate accounts 40% deposit; 1% Setup Fee; upfront GH₵900 Facility Fee; 3 fixed repayments

Escalation Protocol: Fixed tier supports a maximum automated financed balance of GH₵50,000. Requests exceeding this threshold require the Insurance Partner's Relationship Manager (RM) Approval.

4. Governance, Repayment & Cancellation Treatment

Repayment collection: Repayments may be administered directly by Moovon or, where agreed in writing, collected by the insurance partner on behalf of Moovon and remitted in accordance with partnership procedures.

Cancellation treatment: Where a policy is cancelled following payment default, applicable charges and outstanding obligations are deducted first. Any remaining unused premium is retained or applied in accordance with the Partnership Agreement and policy terms.

Fraud Controls & Security: Mandatory Ghana Card identification, live biometric selfie matching, automated audit trails, and reconciliation dashboards ensure end-to-end operational integrity.
