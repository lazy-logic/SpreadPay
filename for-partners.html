For Insurers, Brokers & Regulated Financial Institutions

Let’s Move Insurance Forward.

Partner with Moovon to make eligible annual motor insurance premiums more accessible through a technology-enabled premium-financing experience.

Become a Partner Partner Sign In

Insurance policies are provided and underwritten by participating insurance companies licensed by the National Insurance Commission of Ghana. Premium financing is provided in partnership with licensed and regulated financial institutions.

The Proposition

One Annual Premium. Flexible Customer Payments.

Following successful completion of the applicable SpreadPay™ transaction, the annual premium is settled to the insurer in accordance with the partnership arrangement. Eligible customers manage their SpreadPay payments separately over the applicable period.

Annual Premium Settlement

Once the applicable SpreadPay transaction completes successfully, the annual premium is settled to the insurer in accordance with the partnership arrangement.

Customer Flexibility

Eligible customers manage their SpreadPay payments separately over the applicable period, under the terms of their own SpreadPay agreement.

Digital Administration

Moovon provides the technology, customer journey and account administration that connect insurance with premium financing.

Roles are kept distinct. Participating insurance companies provide and underwrite the insurance policy. Licensed and regulated financial institutions provide or participate in the applicable premium-financing arrangement. Moovon is not an insurer and does not handle insurance claims — policy and claim matters remain with the customer’s participating insurer.
Partnership Types

Who We Work With

SpreadPay™ connects regulated and authorised participants around one thing: the customer’s own choice of participating insurer.

Insurance Companies

Participating insurers provide and underwrite eligible motor insurance policies, and can offer SpreadPay as a payment route for eligible customers.

Brokers & Authorised Agents

Brokers and authorised agents can introduce SpreadPay to eligible customers through their existing participating insurer relationships.

Licensed & Regulated Financial Institutions

Regulated financial institutions provide or participate in the applicable premium-financing arrangement alongside Moovon and insurance partners.

A Connected Ecosystem

  1. Customer

    Chooses a participating insurer and asks for Moovon SpreadPay™.

  2. Insurance Partner

    Participating insurer, broker or authorised agent. The insurer provides and underwrites the policy.

  3. Moovon SpreadPay™

    Technology, customer journey, account administration and the payment experience.

  4. Licensed & Regulated Financial Institution

    Provides or participates in the applicable premium-financing arrangement.

The customer chooses the participating insurer. Moovon does not choose an insurance company for the customer, and does not rank or recommend one insurer over another.

High-Level Overview

How the Partnership Works

A high-level view of how a SpreadPay™ partnership is set up and runs. Detailed operational procedures are shared with approved partners through the Partner Portal.

  1. 1

    Partner Onboarding

    Moovon and the partner agree eligible products and the operating arrangements for the partnership.

  2. 2

    Partner Access

    Approved users receive access to the secure partner environment and the briefing materials for their teams.

  3. 3

    Customer Access

    Eligible customers can request SpreadPay™ through participating partner channels.

  4. 4

    Settlement & Administration

    Following successful completion of the transaction, the annual premium is settled to the insurer per the partnership arrangement, and Moovon administers the customer’s SpreadPay account.

SpreadPay™ Product Capability

Flexible structures for different premium levels, from lower-value private motor premiums through to larger commercial and fleet premiums. Current parameters for each structure are shared with approved partners.

  • SpreadPay Essential™ For eligible lower-value annual premiums.
  • SpreadPay Flex™ For eligible mid-to-higher value annual premiums.
  • SpreadPay Fixed™ Structured arrangements for eligible larger commercial and fleet premiums.
Operational detail stays behind Partner Sign In. Product rates, fees, credit rules, reconciliation, settlement procedures, internal controls and other operational guidance are not published on this website. They are made available to approved partners through the secure Partner Portal.
