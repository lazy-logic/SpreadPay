Let’s Move Insurance Forward.

Moovon SpreadPay™ helps participating insurance partners give eligible customers greater flexibility in how they fund their annual motor insurance premiums — while the applicable annual premium is settled to the insurer in accordance with the SpreadPay arrangement.

The Proposition

One Annual Premium. Flexible Customer Payments.

Following successful completion of the applicable SpreadPay™ transaction, the annual premium is settled to the insurer in accordance with the partnership arrangement. Eligible customers manage their SpreadPay payments separately over the applicable period.

Annual Premium Settlement

Once the applicable SpreadPay transaction completes successfully, the annual premium is settled to the insurer in accordance with the partnership arrangement.

Customer Flexibility

Eligible customers manage their SpreadPay payments separately over the applicable period, under the terms of their own SpreadPay agreement.

Digital Administration

Moovon provides the technology, customer journey and account administration that connect insurance with premium financing.

Who We Work With

A Connected Ecosystem

  1. Customer

    Chooses a participating insurer and asks for Moovon SpreadPay™.

  2. Insurance Partner

    Participating insurer, broker or authorised agent. The insurer provides and underwrites the policy.

  3. Moovon SpreadPay™

    Technology, customer journey, account administration and the payment experience.

  4. Licensed & Regulated Financial Institution

    Provides or participates in the applicable premium-financing arrangement.

The customer chooses the participating insurer. Moovon does not choose an insurance company for the customer, and does not rank or recommend one insurer over another.
